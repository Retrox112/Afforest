About Afforest

Anonymous & Decentralized Tree Mapping

Afforest is a community platform designed to log, track, and monitor the growth of trees worldwide. Whether it's an ancient oak in an old forest or a sapling planted today in an urban neighborhood, Afforest lets anyone document nature with ease.
